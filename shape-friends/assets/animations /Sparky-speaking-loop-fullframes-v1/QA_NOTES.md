# Speaking loop QA

This package contains all 14 numbered 512×512 transparent frames, a contact sheet, transparent APNG preview, and timing manifest. It is **a review draft, not production-ready animation**. Frames 000 and 013 are pixel-identical to the approved neutral. Intermediate frames were cropped from two image-generated drawing sheets. Their identity, seat and wand pivots, and mouth registration are not pixel-locked; the upper-to-lower-row transition may pop. The exact mouth/seat/wand coordinates in the manifest are approximate reference points and require measured replacement after redrawing. No separate perch or travelling star is baked in.

Art pass required: correct frames 001–012 against the master, repair any clipped glove/wand details, lock mouth and seat, redraw smooth in-betweens, and regenerate preview before game integration.
