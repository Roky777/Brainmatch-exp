Shape Friends · Grade 1 shape content. Practice: every turn is yours. Beat Sparky: a pair scores one point and earns another turn. A miss passes the turn. No timer. Difficulty is not a grade level.

Main cues use local generated voice clips; other lines use device speech. Captions always work.

Keyboard: Tab, then Enter or Space. Tap Sparky for help in Practice. Escape pauses. Home returns to game selection. Completed sets are saved on this device.
