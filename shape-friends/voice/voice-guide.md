# Character direction

Name: Sparky. Identity: a small enthusiastic flame friend, sometimes a friendly rival. Register: simple conversational English for children in classes 1–3. Short sentences, warm humour, occasional “Hmm” and “Oops”. Never insults, pressures, shames or claims to hear the child. Under challenge he becomes thoughtful, not aggressive. Winning celebrates the child; losing invites another game without pressure.

Reactions fire at meaningful events, not on an idle timer. Match/miss variants rotate. First-card prompts occur every other attempt only when another local line is not playing. Practice keeps the child's turn; a tap on Sparky gives a memory-based hint. Existing drawn wave, think/point and cheer clips express state changes. No speech recognition, generated conversation or phoneme lip-sync is implemented.

Sparky is an encouraging playmate. Simple vocabulary, short sentences, warm delight. Mistakes are shared discoveries, never scolded. Use a gentle smile in delivery; do not artificially speed or pitch-shift the files. These initial rendered clips use the generator's delicate preset, not a custom character-trained voice. Listening approval remains with the user.
